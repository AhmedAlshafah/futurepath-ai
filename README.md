# FuturePath AI

An AI-powered career guidance platform developed for the SDC 2026 Hackathon.

## Problem

Students and job seekers often struggle to identify suitable careers, understand their skill gaps, and find relevant learning opportunities.

## Our Solution

FuturePath AI aims to help users:

- Discover careers matching their skills and interests.
- Identify missing skills for their target careers.
- Receive personalized learning recommendations.
- Understand how different careers contribute to the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
